# FilterAniX Style Presets Guide

FilterAniX includes preconfigured artistic style presets in `styles.json`.

---

## Available Presets

### 1. `anime_creator` (Default)
- **Description**: Clean Japanese anime aesthetic with fine ink contours, multi-level cel-shading, and warm studio rim lighting.
- **Line Weight**: 1.4
- **Shading Levels**: 6
- **Warmth**: 0.70

### 2. `clean_illustration`
- **Description**: Crisp editorial vector illustration with flat tone layering and distinct silhouette definition.
- **Line Weight**: 1.2
- **Shading Levels**: 8
- **Warmth**: 0.50

### 3. `comic`
- **Description**: Bold Western graphic novel inking with punchy shadows and saturated color tones.
- **Line Weight**: 2.0
- **Shading Levels**: 4
- **Warmth**: 0.40

### 4. `watercolor`
- **Description**: Soft painterly gouache wash with subtle edge blooming and delicate line highlights.
- **Line Weight**: 0.9
- **Shading Levels**: 10
- **Warmth**: 0.80

### 5. `manga`
- **Description**: High-contrast monochrome Japanese manga screentone styling with deep ink blacks.
- **Line Weight**: 1.8
- **Shading Levels**: 3
- **Warmth**: 0.0 (Monochrome)
